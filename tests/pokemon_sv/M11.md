## Proposed work

1. Require an observed scrub-to-rinse transition before entering the rinse stage.
   On scrub timeout, return failure or enter explicit bounded recovery immediately.
2. Verify the Pokémon is accessible when washing starts; investigate positioning
   relative to picnic furniture. Capture cursor coverage and actual game feedback
   before choosing movement, camera or target-selection changes.
3. Define a reliable progress observation. Button presence and issued A/stick
   commands alone are insufficient. If no usable intermediate signal exists, use
   a bounded attempt window rather than claim to detect scrub progress.
4. Trial a 30–60 second no-progress window, one reposition/re-entry recovery and a
   total attempt budget. Tune from successful and obstructed runs; these values
   are proposals, not validated defaults. Return control to M4/M6 retry/fallback
   handling when recovery is exhausted. Do not repeat identical failed geometry.
5. Keep minigame transition/completion evidence separate from quest success:
   confirm completion on the board before awarding quest credit. Preserve safe
   picnic exit, cancellation and shiny-stop behavior.
