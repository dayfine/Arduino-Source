Status: observed problem and proposed work; no implementation yet. This is a
separate milestone from M7 (quest-board reads) and M8 (startup egg inventory).
The dominant cost in this example is the Auto Battle route's movement/recovery
policy, not board OCR.

## What the code establishes

Observed code paths are relative to SerialPrograms/Source/VideoGames/PokemonSV:

- Programs/Farming/PokemonSV_BlueberryQuests.cpp, quest_auto_battle(): fly to
  Chargestone, wait for spawns, follow a fixed short path, and clear in front.
- Programs/ShinyHunting/PokemonSV_LetsGoTools.cpp,
  use_lets_go_to_clear_in_front(): send Let's Go once; after the supplied move/
  wait sequence, leave the clear loop after six seconds without a detected kill.
- The route then unconditionally returns to Central Plaza, attempts healing,
  and performs its map/flight reset. The target count is checked only at the
  next outer loop iteration. No local search/reposition cycle is attempted first.
- The outer loop requires ten (or thirty) newly tracked detections, regardless
  of any progress already on the quest. It has no overall duration or sustained
  no-progress limit. M4/M6's outer retry/failure handling cannot intervene while
  this route continues internally.

The logs prove low yield and expensive cycling. They do not establish the root
cause of low yield. Inspect a full cave visit visually to distinguish spawn
placement/density, route/camera position, unreachable or refused targets, and
missed/false defeat detections before changing navigation or detector thresholds.

## Proposed improvements

1. Add route diagnostics: time, detections gained per pass, time since progress,
   and an explicit reason for each reposition, heal, reset or exit. Pair one full
   recorded visit with these events to establish why targets are being missed.
2. Clear another nearby area/reposition and resend Let's Go before leaving the
   cave. Prefer a verified, bounded local search over repeating the same short
   approach. Keep the movement area safe and handle incidental battles.
3. End clearing promptly at the target; do not require the rest of a pass or an
   unconditional heal/flight cycle first. Reach a safe overworld state and verify
   completion on the board before granting quest credit.
4. Heal/recover when a verified condition calls for it (HP, refusal, abnormal
   state), rather than after every small cluster. Identify the purpose of the
   current reset sequence and retain any necessary positioning/spawn reset.
5. Add both a no-progress budget and a total attempt budget. Exhausting either
   must return a failed attempt/recovery outcome that the farmer can handle with
   its existing retry/fallback policy. Never loop indefinitely if no enemies spawn
   or the lead cannot attack. Set budgets from measured traces, not an assumption
   that all ten enemies are always available in one visit.
6. If reliable board progress becomes available through M7, use verified remaining
   work instead of always demanding ten fresh detections on a partially completed
   quest. Until then, do not invent a remaining count or assume sound detections
   prove completion. Avoid replacing travel overhead with excessive board reads.

Changes to shared Let's Go helpers must preserve other callers' behavior unless
they opt into the new route policy. Preserve shiny-stop and missing-audio handling,
and give interrupted recovery a defined exit state.

## Acceptance criteria and validation

- Native C++ UnitTest/UnitTestDatabase cases exercise the production route policy
  through injectable game actions: target reached mid-clear, partial progress,
  zero-yield pass, local reposition, low-HP/refusal recovery, missed/unknown
  feedback, and total/no-progress budget exhaustion. No Python/source-extraction
  harnesses or test-only branches in production sources.
- Once the target is reached, no needless additional clear/heal/reset cycle is
  scheduled. Completion still requires a valid board observation.
- A low-yield or zero-yield route terminates within its configured bounds and
  returns control to outer retry/fallback handling; it does not count as success.
- Local build and real Switch validation are required. Capture a full cave visit,
  lead/HP state, per-pass gains, travel/healing duration, board progress and
  completion, and visible BP before/after.
- Compare repeated baseline and changed runs under similar starting conditions.
  Report median completion time, visits, yield per visit and recovery overhead.
  The examples above are baselines, not a promised one-trip or fixed-seconds SLA.
