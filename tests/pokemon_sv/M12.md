## Shared mechanism and open hypotheses

In the observed M5 production sources:

- BlueberryQuests.cpp, quest_sneak_up(), and BlueberryCatchPhoto.cpp,
  quest_catch_navi()/quest_photo_navi(), use the same timed approach from Savanna
  Plaza to Pride Rock, with a Switch1-specific final adjustment.
- They do not establish target presence and framing before committing to the
  throw/photo sequence. Catch waits 25 seconds for a battle menu; Sneak Up waits
  15 seconds. On no battle they return to plaza; catch/sneak also roll the date
  to attempt a fixed-spawn reset. Those resets did not eliminate the failures.
- A photo shutter command or prompt timeout is not proof of a usable target.
  `Photo not taken.` appears even on photo dispatches later confirmed complete;
  do not use that message alone as the failure classification.

Ranked investigation questions, not established causes:

1. Target availability/respawn: if absent, a recorded arrival should show no
   eligible target; validated respawn or an alternate target should restore it.
2. Arrival/camera drift or wrong framing: if present but off target, verified
   positioning and target lock should restore encounter/photo results.
3. Interaction/state detection: if target and framing are correct but logs miss
   the event, correlate captured battle/photo UI with detector decisions.

Do not tune timing or detector thresholds from the log warning alone. A bounded
recording of one complete approach is needed to distinguish these predictions.

## Proposed work and acceptance

- Share navigation where appropriate, then verify the prerequisites separately
  for capture, an unnoticed rear approach, and a quest-eligible photograph.
- If no eligible target is established, use a bounded local search/reposition or
  validated reset/alternate target. Do not repeat the same failed geometry
  indefinitely, and do not assume a date change proves the target respawned.
- Fail explicitly when the recovery/time budget ends. M4/M6 must retain the
  exhausted quest state across batches and apply the configured fallback.
- For Catch Any and biome photo, investigate a nearby available eligible target
  instead of requiring the same fixed spawn. Type/rear-approach requirements
  still need their own verification.
- Native C++ UnitTest/UnitTestDatabase cases must exercise production decisions
  through injected actions: target absent, wrong/uncertain target, reacquisition,
  missing battle entry, photo ambiguity, recovery exhaustion and cancellation.
  No Python/source-extraction tests or test-only production branches.
- Build locally and record live target-present/absent attempts on the actual
  console. Compare arrival, acquisition, encounter/photo result, confirmed board
  changes and total duration. Preserve shiny-stop and safe overworld recovery.

Source/evidence references and exact dispatch times are in the daily audit's
CSV and filtered event log. M12 addresses target acquisition; M9 covers Auto
Battle, M11 washing, and M13 navigation recovery. No blanket unsupported filter
or automatic spending-policy change is proposed.
