## Reward model and evidence levels

Reward/cost schedule checked on 2026-10-21 against
[Bulbapedia's quest table](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Blueberry_Quest).
For the observed types: Auto-10, TM, Travel and Sneak Up pay 20; in-flight, swimming and biome
photos pay 30; type-specific catches pay 40; washing pays 100; the three-filling
sandwich pays 130; hatching pays 200 BP. Rerolls cost 10 for blue and 50 for red.
This is a rule-based model applied to observed identities, not OCR of reward
numbers. Another consulted table (Serebii) disagrees on TM/washing, so its reward
values were not mixed into this model. Future balance snapshots should validate
the rule mapping against this save's actual displayed values.

The [event ledger](fixtures/bbq-bp-ledger-2026-10-21.csv) records every included
income/expense identity, time, amount, basis and confidence; the
[run summary](fixtures/bbq-bp-runs-2026-10-21.csv) preserves per-run calculations.
Underlying OCR/route events remain in the daily audit fixture.

- Income: infer the quest type from the observed board identity that disappeared
  during a successful dispatch; use every supported disappearance when a single
  action completes multiple quests. Do not infer reward from a shared route
  label such as Catch Any/Normal/Fire, whose possible quests pay different amounts.
- M5 expense: compare the revalidation/post-payment boards immediately preceding
  each verified replacement. Classify the removed identity and apply its color's
  cost. One transaction is one expense, not one expense per menu probe/read.
- R06 expense: the 10 BP model is additionally reconciled with the retained
  11560 → 11550 screenshots. No other per-run balance reconciliation is available.
- Interruptions/timeouts: no income is booked without an observed completion.
  A quest might finish before the next board check or be replaced by the same
  enum, so the estimate can omit actual income. OCR misclassification can also
  overstate it; this is not a mathematical lower bound or confidence interval.
- BP/hour uses the same banner-to-stop durations as the quest-velocity audit.
  No external spending/earnings or progress between program runs is included.

For future trials, record expected gross, expected reroll spending and expected
net alongside the directly observed balance delta. A mismatch should trigger
whole-board/event reconciliation, not silently alter the reward model or assume
that every missing counter increment was unpaid.
